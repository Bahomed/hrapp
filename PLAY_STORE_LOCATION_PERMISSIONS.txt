LOCATION ACCESS - BACKGROUND LOCATION FEATURE (Main Feature)
------------------------------------------------------------
Feature Name: Attendance Clock-In/Clock-Out Verification

Description:
The app uses location access during attendance clock-in and clock-out operations to verify that employees are at their designated workplace location. When an employee attempts to clock in at the start of their shift or clock out at the end, the app checks their GPS location against the company's registered workplace coordinates to ensure they are physically present at the correct location. This prevents fraudulent attendance submissions and ensures accurate time tracking.

Background location access is required because employees may switch to other apps or lock their device while the clock-in/out process completes. The location verification happens only during active attendance operations and is not collected continuously. Location data is used solely for attendance verification and is not tracked or stored for any other purpose.

The feature has a user interface where employees see their clock-in/clock-out screen with location verification status. Users are informed when location is being accessed, and the feature can only be triggered by explicit user action (tapping the clock-in or clock-out button).
