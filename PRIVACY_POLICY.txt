PRIVACY POLICY FOR INJAZAT HR APP

Last Updated: October 9, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Injazat Software ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Injazat HR mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Personal Information
We collect the following types of personal information:
- Mobile number (for login authentication)
- Full name and employee details
- Employee ID and workplace information
- Profile photographs and biometric face data (for attendance verification)
- Location data (for attendance check-in/check-out purposes)
- Contact information
- Employment information (job title, department, manager, etc.)
- Salary and payroll information

1.2 Attendance and Location Data
- GPS location coordinates when clocking in/out
- Network information (WiFi/cellular connection details)
- Face recognition data for identity verification during attendance
- Camera access for capturing attendance photos
- Attendance timestamps and history

1.3 Device Information
- Device model and operating system version
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push notifications
- App usage data and preferences
- Language and theme preferences
- Local storage data

1.4 Work-Related Information
- Leave requests and approval history
- Permission requests
- Loan requests
- Letter requests
- Work schedule and calendar information
- Holiday information
- Payroll details and history
- Documents uploaded to the system
- Manager and subordinate relationships

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

2.1 Core HR Functions
- Employee authentication and account management
- Attendance tracking and verification
- Processing leave, permission, loan, and letter requests
- Managing work schedules and calendars
- Calculating and displaying payroll information
- Document management and storage

2.2 Face Recognition
- Verifying employee identity during clock-in/clock-out
- Preventing attendance fraud
- Maintaining accurate attendance records

2.3 Location Services
- Verifying employee location during attendance check-in/check-out
- Ensuring employees are at authorized locations
- Displaying location information on maps

2.4 Communication
- Sending push notifications about:
  - Attendance reminders
  - Leave request status updates
  - Important announcements
  - System notifications
  - Manager approvals

2.5 App Functionality
- Personalizing user experience (theme, language)
- Maintaining app sessions
- Storing user preferences locally
- Providing offline functionality

3. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

3.1 Local Storage
- We use GetStorage to store certain data locally on your device
- This includes authentication tokens, user preferences, and cached data
- Local data is encrypted and protected by your device security

3.2 Server Storage
- Employee data is stored on secure servers managed by your organization
- The App connects to your organization's workspace URL
- All data transmissions use secure HTTPS connections

3.3 Biometric Data
- Face recognition data is processed for attendance verification
- Facial embeddings may be stored for comparison purposes
- This data is used solely for identity verification

3.4 Security Measures
- Authentication tokens are used to secure all API communications
- Bearer token authentication protects all data exchanges
- Face recognition is performed using Google ML Kit for enhanced security

4. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

4.1 Within Your Organization
- Your data is shared with:
  - Your direct manager(s) for approval workflows
  - HR department for personnel management
  - Payroll department for salary processing
  - Authorized administrators

4.2 Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services:
- Firebase Cloud Messaging: For push notifications
- Firebase Core: For app infrastructure
- Google ML Kit: For face detection and recognition
- Location services: For GPS coordinates
- Camera services: For capturing attendance photos

4.3 We Do NOT:
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your data for marketing purposes
- Share data outside your organization without authorization

5. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

5.1 Access and Control
- You can view your personal information within the App
- You can update certain profile information
- You can view your attendance, leave, and payroll history

5.2 Permissions
You can manage the following permissions:
- Camera access (required for face verification)
- Location access (required for attendance)
- Notification permissions (for alerts and updates)
- Storage access (for document uploads)

5.3 Data Retention
- Your data is retained as long as you are an employee
- Data retention policies are determined by your organization
- Attendance and payroll records are maintained for legal compliance

6. LOCATION DATA

6.1 Collection
- We collect precise location data when you clock in/out
- Location is captured using GPS, WiFi, and cellular networks
- We also collect network information to verify connectivity

6.2 Purpose
- Verify you are at an authorized work location
- Display location on attendance records
- Provide geocoded address information
- Ensure attendance accuracy

6.3 Control
- You must grant location permission to use attendance features
- Location is only collected during clock-in/clock-out actions
- You can revoke location permission, but attendance features may not work

7. CAMERA AND FACE RECOGNITION

7.1 Camera Access
- Camera access is required for:
  - Face verification during attendance
  - Uploading profile photos
  - Document scanning and upload

7.2 Face Recognition
- We use Google ML Kit for face detection
- Face images are processed to create mathematical representations
- These representations are compared with stored data for verification
- Face data is used solely for identity verification

7.3 Quality and Privacy
- Face images are checked for quality before processing
- Images must meet minimum size and clarity requirements
- Processing occurs securely on device and server

8. PUSH NOTIFICATIONS

8.1 Firebase Cloud Messaging
- We use FCM to send push notifications
- Your FCM token is stored on our servers
- Tokens are updated automatically when they change

8.2 Types of Notifications
- Attendance reminders
- Request status updates (leave, permission, loan, letter)
- Announcements from management
- System alerts
- Approval notifications for managers

8.3 Control
- You can disable notifications in device settings
- You can manage notification preferences in the App settings
- Disabling notifications may affect your ability to receive important updates

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

This App is intended for use by employees of organizations. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 18. This is a professional HR application for workplace use only.

- Sending notifications for significant changes

Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
